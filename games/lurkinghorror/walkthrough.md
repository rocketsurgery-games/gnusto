# The Lurking Horror Walkthrough

Based on Doug Bolden's walkthrough from [wyrmis.com](https://www.wyrmis.com/games/if/wtthelurkinghorror.html) (Release 221).

## Key Items

| Item | Location | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| Flashlight | Terminal room (on hacker) | Illumination in dark areas |
| Master key | Trade with hacker | Opens locked doors |
| Coke bottle | Vending machine | Prevents fatal sleep |
| Gloves | Alchemy lab | Electrical/chemical protection |
| Boots | Brown basement | Electrical protection |
| Crowbar | Brown basement | Prying things open |
| Knife | Alchemy lab | Cutting |
| Axe | Maintenance closet | Cutting cables/wax |
| Flask | Chemistry lab | Carrying liquid nitrogen |
| Smooth stone | Dream sequence | Final weapon |
| Mummified hand | Brown dome bathtub | Maze guide (after revival) |

## Walkthrough

### Part 1: Terminal Room & Dream Sequence

1. Wake up in terminal room with the hacker
2. Login to PC, read your assignment
3. Fall asleep and experience the nightmare
4. In the dream: get the smooth stone from the altar
5. Wake up - the hacker will now help you

### Part 2: Getting the Master Key

1. Go to the kitchen (east from hallway)
2. Open the microwave, put food inside
3. Set timer to 5:00, turn on microwave
4. Wait for food to cook
5. Take the hot container back to the hacker
6. Give food to hacker - he gives you the master key

### Part 3: Basement Exploration

1. Take the elevator down to the basement
2. Get the crowbar from Brown basement
3. Get the gloves from the alchemy lab
4. Get the knife
5. Use the forklift to access the "Tomb of the Unknown Tool"

### Part 4: Chemistry Lab & The Ceremony

1. Show the strange paper to the professor
2. This triggers the timed ceremony sequence
3. When the pentagram appears, cut the line with the knife
4. Escape before being trapped
5. The professor is defeated; get the flask

### Part 5: The Hand

1. Go to the Brown Building dome
2. Find the bathtub with the mummified hand
3. Take the hand
4. Revive it in the vat (chemistry lab)
5. Put the hyrax ring on the hand

### Part 6: Steam Tunnels

1. Navigate the elevator shaft puzzle
2. Create a chain-secured hook system
3. Access the steam tunnels
4. Use the axe to deal with the maintenance machine
5. Apply wax to the floor to stop it

### Part 7: The Maze (Lair)

1. Enter the maze with the reanimated hand
2. The hand points the way through
3. Use liquid nitrogen (in flask) to freeze the slime curtain
4. Pour nitrogen three times to shatter it

### Part 8: Final Battle

1. Find the high-voltage cable in the water
2. Wear gloves and boots for protection
3. Cut the cable with the axe (3 hits)
4. Plug the cable into the socket
5. This transforms the mass into the creature
6. Throw the smooth stone at the creature (twice)
7. Victory!

## Tips

- **Inventory management**: You can only carry so much; drop items you don't need
- **Battery life**: The flashlight batteries will run out eventually
- **Timing**: Several sequences are timed - don't dawdle
- **Save often**: Many ways to die in this game
- **The hand**: Once revived with the ring, it guides you through the maze by pointing
