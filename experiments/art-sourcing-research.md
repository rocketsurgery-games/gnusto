# Art Sourcing Research

Research into finding contract artists for hand-drawn reference art (rooms, characters, objects) in a dark/moody graphic novel style.

## Best Places to Find Artists

### Portfolio-first platforms (browse work, then reach out)

- **[ArtStation](https://www.artstation.com/channels/comic_art)** — Dedicated Comic Art and Game Art channels. Browse by style, contact artists directly. Best starting point for finding the right aesthetic match.
- **[Behance](https://www.behance.net/hire/comic-artists)** — Similar portfolio browsing. Adobe-affiliated, strong with digital illustrators.

### Curated marketplaces (post a job, get applicants)

- **[Reedsy](https://reedsy.com/hire/us/book-illustrators/comics-graphic-novels/)** — Curated directory of graphic novel illustrators. Higher-end, artists have published credits (DC, Fantagraphics, etc.).
- **[Work With Indies](https://www.workwithindies.com/)** — Job board specifically for indie game dev. Artists understand game asset workflows.

### General freelance platforms

- **[Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/hire/comic-artists/)** — Widest range of price points. Built-in contracts, milestones, escrow.
- **[Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/hire/graphic-novel-illustration)** — Lower barrier to entry, more variable quality, but some genuinely good artists.

### Reddit communities

- **r/HungryArtists** — Artists post "[For Hire]" with portfolios, or you post "[Hiring]" with your brief. Very active.
- **r/GameDevClassifieds** — Game-dev-specific hiring board.
- **r/artcommissions** — General commission community.

## Rough Pricing (per-piece, not per-page)

| Asset type | Approximate range |
|---|---|
| Character portrait (half-body) | $75 - $250 |
| Full character illustration | $150 - $500 |
| Environment / room scene | $200 - $600 |
| Simple object illustration | $30 - $100 |

Rates vary enormously by artist experience and region. Eastern European and South/Southeast Asian artists often charge 40-60% less for comparable quality.

## Style Notes

For the Gnusto aesthetic (dark blues, serif fonts, Lurking Horror vibe), look for:

- "Semi-realistic" or "painterly comic" style — pure line-art comics won't match the web UI mood
- Something between comic ink work and painted illustration — darker, more atmospheric graphic novel feel
- Search terms: "dark fantasy comic", "graphic novel horror", "moody game art"

## Suggested Approach

1. **Start on ArtStation** — find 5-10 artists whose style resonates, check commission availability.
2. **Paid test piece** — commission one character + one room from 2-3 finalists ($150-300 each). See how their style works as reference art for FLUX composition.
3. **Define the asset list first** — count rooms, characters, key objects to scope total budget.
