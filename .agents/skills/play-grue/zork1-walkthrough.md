# Zork I — a natural-language walkthrough

A spoiler-level guide to the interesting paths of Zork I, written as **intents
you can issue more or less verbatim** to the parse-only harness (and vary as you
like). It is the spine for a playthrough; improvise exploration and the odd
mistake around it. One local intent per turn — the parser carries out implied
prerequisites but won't pathfind across the map or solve a puzzle for you.

## The goal

Recover the **nineteen treasures** of the Great Underground Empire and deposit
each in the **trophy case** in the living room. When the last one is banked, an
inscription reveals a secret path; go to **West of House**, take the path
**southwest into the Stone Barrow**, and step inside to win.

## Getting underground

- West of House: read the leaflet in the mailbox for flavor. The front door is
  boarded — a dead end. Head **around the house** (north, then east) to the
  back, where a **kitchen window is ajar**: force it open and climb in.
- Kitchen → **west into the living room**. Take the **brass lantern** and the
  **sword**, and **turn the lantern on** (most of the underground is pitch dark,
  and a grue eats anyone who lingers in the dark).
- **Move the rug** to uncover a trap door, **open it, and climb down** to the
  cellar. It bars behind you — you'll return to the surface by other routes.

## The troll and the first treasure

- Cellar → **north** to the Troll Room. A troll blocks the passages: **attack it
  with the sword** (a couple of blows kills it; bare hands are suicide).
- For an easy first treasure, from the cellar take the **south crawlway → east
  into the Gallery** and **take the painting**. Carry it home **up the chimney**
  from the **Studio** (north of the Gallery) — but the chimney only takes the
  lantern plus **one** item, so **drop the sword** first. Up the chimney puts you
  back in the kitchen; go to the living room, **open the trophy case and put the
  painting inside**.

## The dam and the reservoir

- From the Troll Room, **east** through the East-West Passage to the **Round
  Room** (a hub), then work toward **Flood Control Dam #3**.
- In the **Maintenance Room** (off the Dam Lobby) is a wall of buttons and a
  **wrench**. **Press the yellow button** (it energizes the control panel — the
  green bubble glows), and **take the wrench**. Back at the dam, **turn the bolt
  with the wrench**: the sluice gates open and the reservoir drains, revealing a
  **trunk of jewels** on the bed. (Avoid the **blue button** — it floods the
  room.)

## The temple and the exorcism

- The temple area holds the **bell**, **black book**, and **candles**; grab the
  **matchbook** from the Dam Lobby to light them. Descend through the altar/Tiny
  Cave to the **Entrance to Hades**, where evil spirits bar the gate.
- Perform the ceremony **in this order**: **ring the bell** (it paralyzes the
  wraiths), **light the candles** (needs the matches), then **read the black
  book** — the spirits flee. Go **in** to the Land of the Dead and take the
  **crystal skull**.

## The maze, the cyclops, and the thief

- West of the Troll Room lies the **maze** ("twisty little passages, all
  alike"). Its rooms look identical, so follow the route by direction: from the
  Troll Room **W, S, E, up, SW, E, S, SE** brings you to the **Cyclops Room**
  (grab the bag of coins and skeleton key on the way).
- The cyclops blocks the stairs. **Say "Odysseus"** (his father's nemesis) and
  he flees in terror, knocking down the east wall — which also opens a shortcut
  back to the living room.
- **Up** the stairs is the **thief's Treasure Room**. The clean solve: **give
  the thief the jewel-encrusted egg** (fetch it first from the bird's nest **up
  the tree** on the Forest Path) — his deft fingers open it without wrecking the
  clockwork canary inside, and he's engrossed. Now **attack him with the sword**;
  engrossed, he falls fast. He drops his loot and the **chalice** is safe to
  take. (Wind the intact **canary** back in the forest to charm a songbird into
  dropping the **brass bauble**.)

## The coal mine and the diamond

- Beyond the mirror rooms lies the coal mine. **Warning:** the **gas room** is
  full of coal gas — carrying **any open flame** (the torch or lit candles) in
  there is instantly fatal. Light your way with the **electric lantern** only.
- Deep in, use the **basket/dumbwaiter** in the Shaft Room: load it with the
  **coal**, the **screwdriver**, and (for light) the **torch**, lower it, then
  **squeeze through the narrow passage empty-handed** to retrieve it below. Put
  the **coal in the machine**, close the lid, and **turn the switch with the
  screwdriver** — the coal is pressed into a **diamond**.

## The river and the rainbow

- At the **Dam Base**, **inflate the boat with the air pump**, then **get in**
  (leave sharp things behind — a blade would puncture it). **Go down** to ride
  the Frigid River past treasures; land on a beach before the falls (going over
  is fatal).
- At Aragain Falls, **wave the sceptre** to make the **rainbow** solid and claim
  the **pot of gold**.

## Banking and the endgame

- Ferry every treasure back to the living-room **trophy case** and put each one
  inside. Useful returns to the surface: the **chimney** from the Studio and the
  cyclops **shortcut** (the smashed east wall → Strange Passage → living room).
- When the last treasure is deposited, the case reveals the secret. Go to **West
  of House**, head **southwest** to the Stone Barrow, and **go in** — victory.

## Hazards (natural mistakes to survive)

- **Darkness/grue:** never linger in an unlit room without a lit light source.
- **Gas room:** no open flame — use the electric lantern there.
- **The falls:** land before Aragain Falls; don't ride over.
- **The blue button** at the dam floods the Maintenance Room.
- Opening the **egg yourself** wrecks the canary — let the thief do it.
